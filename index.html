Hi, we’re Cool Kids University

We make learning a little more fun and a lot more personal.

Kids learn best when they’re curious, comfortable, and a little bit excited. So we start with stories they’ll love, then add simple ways to keep the learning going.

The kids in our stories come from all kinds of families, cultures, and neighborhoods, with all kinds of personalities and abilities. When children see themselves in a story, they lean in. When they meet someone different, they get curious.

Everything we make fits together, so a book you read at bedtime can turn into a video, a game, or a great conversation the next day.

How it started

“Hey, Miss CiCi!”

I’d hear that when I showed up at my children’s schools. I’m CiCi Caver, a mom of four and the founder of Cool Kids University. I volunteered, hung out with the kids at recess, and for a short while, I was a substitute teacher. Somewhere along the way, I became the “fun mom.”

At recess, I got to know the kids a little differently. I heard their jokes, listened to their stories, and noticed things about their personalities that were easy to miss in a classroom. Those were the kids I wanted to see in books and games.

Cool Kids University started with my children, Chloe and Carter. The characters are loosely based on them and the kids I’ve met over the years. The teachers in our stories are inspired by real educators I’ve met, too. My hope is that every child can find someone here who reminds them of a friend, a favorite teacher, or even themselves.

Conversation starters

Easy ways to get kids talking.

Simple questions that help grownups talk with kids about feelings, kindness, and what they’re learning.

Good for parents, grandparents, and caregivers

Try these tonight

Pick one or two at dinner, in the car, or at bedtime.

About feelings

  • What made you smile today?
  • Was anything hard today?
  • When did you feel proud of yourself?

About friends

  • Who did you play with today?
  • How can you tell when a friend is sad?
  • What makes someone a good friend?

About kindness

  • What’s one kind thing you saw today?
  • How could we help someone this week?
  • How do you feel when someone is kind to you?

About learning

  • What’s something new you learned?
  • What do you want to know more about?
  • If you could teach me something, what would it be?

A few tips

Listen more

Let them finish, even if it takes a minute.

Share your answer too

Kids open up more when you go first.

Keep it light

No wrong answers. Just curiosity.
