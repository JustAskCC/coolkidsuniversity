Where stories spark learning.

Learning that feels like play. Stories that feel like home.

Cool Kids University makes books, videos, games, and activities for kids ages 4 to 10. Everything we make is meant to help children learn something new, feel good about who they are, and have fun along the way.

For every kid, and especially for the kids who don’t always get to see themselves in what they’re learning.

Hi, we’re Cool Kids University

We make learning a little more fun and a lot more personal.

Kids learn best when they’re curious, comfortable, and a little bit excited. So we start with stories they’ll love, then add simple ways to keep the learning going.

The kids in our stories come from all kinds of families, cultures, and neighborhoods, with all kinds of personalities and abilities. When children see themselves in a story, they lean in. When they meet someone different, they get curious.

Everything we make fits together, so a book you read at bedtime can turn into a video, a game, or a great conversation the next day.

How we help

Good for kids. Easier for the grownups who love them.

Whether you’re a parent, a teacher, or someone who wants to see kids thrive, here’s what Cool Kids University brings to the table.

  • Stronger reading, math, and science skills, picked up through stories and play
  • More confidence, plus the words to talk about big feelings
  • Stories with kids who look and live like them

For parents and caregivers

  • Screen time you can actually feel good about
  • Easy ways to start real conversations at dinner or bedtime
  • Activities you can print and pull out on a rainy afternoon
  • Stories that honor your family and your culture

For teachers

  • Lessons and slideshows you can use tomorrow morning
  • Materials that look like the kids in your room

For community partners

  • A simple way to bring joyful, culturally rich learning to the kids you serve

  • Something families can keep using at home long after your program ends

Why we do this

Every kid deserves to feel seen and ready to learn.

We want learning to feel welcoming to every child, no matter where they come from.

That means stories that spark curiosity, build confidence, help kids understand their own feelings, and teach them to understand other people too.

We make this for all kids, with extra care for the kids and communities who too often get left out.

What we care about

The things that matter most to us.

Every kid belongs

Kids should get to see all kinds of cultures, families, and abilities in the stories they read.

Feelings matter

Curiosity is the best teacher

How it started

Kids should be able to see themselves in their stories.

What kids read and watch shapes how they see themselves, how they see others, and what they believe they can become.

Cool Kids University started with a simple wish: a learning world that looks more like the kids and families we know. One where different cultures, personalities, and abilities are just a normal part of the story.

When kids feel like they belong, they’re more willing to try, ask questions, and dream big.

Learning is everywhere

At home, around the neighborhood, and in the classroom.

Kids don’t only learn at school, so our stories follow them to all the places they grow.

Family

Brothers and sisters, family traditions, helping out at home, and time with grandparents.

Community

Neighbors, mentors, and favorite places that show kids all the ways people look out for each other.

School

Teachers and classmates, plus the subjects, teamwork, and growing up that happen there.

That way, what kids learn connects to the life they’re already living.
